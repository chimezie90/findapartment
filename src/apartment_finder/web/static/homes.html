How the monthly estimate works

Every monthly figure is an estimate for one buyer, for year 1. It assumes:

  • 5% cash down payment.
  • 80% realkredit loan: 30-year fixed, 4% coupon, bond price 95. The debt is 0.80 × price ÷ 0.95.
  • Contribution fee 0.74% a year on that debt.
  • 15% bank loan at 3.70%, 30 years.
  • Interest tax deduction of about 33% on the first 50,000 kr. a year of interest and fees, and 25% above.
  • Running costs: the listing's monthly owner expenses (ejerudgift) when known. Otherwise estimated property taxes for a new buyer, based on the price.

Cash out is the loan payment after tax plus running costs. Real cost is cash out minus the loan principal you repay, which you keep as equity.

Cash to buy is 5% down + deed fee (0.6% + 1,850 kr.) + mortgage registration (1.25% of both loans) + about 45,000 kr. of other fees.

Price per m² is compared with the municipality's Q2 2026 average (Finans Danmark), where we have one.
